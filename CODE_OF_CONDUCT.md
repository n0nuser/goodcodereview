# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The Covenant is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), and the full text at that link applies.

In short:

- Be respectful and kind. Argue with a card's rule, not with the person who wrote it.
- Give and take feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or publishing other people's private information.
- Maintainers can remove, edit, or reject contributions that break these rules, and can ban the people who make them.

The rules apply in issues, pull requests, and any other space where you speak for the project.
